# Electronics Monitoring Leader proteanTecs Raises $51M in Series D Funding

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/pressroom/electronics-monitoring-leader-proteantecs-raises-51m-in-series-d-funding
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: 2025-09-09

proteanTecs announced closing a $51 million Series D funding round. IAG Capital Partners led the round, with new strategic investors Samsung Catalyst Fund, Arm and Siemens. Existing investors including Addition, Zeev Ventures, Avigdor Willenz Group, MediaTek Innovation Fund, Intel Capital, Porsche Automobil Holding SE and Koch Disruptive Technologies continued support.

The company stated it was founded in 2017, headquartered in Israel, and had offices in the United States, India and Taiwan.
