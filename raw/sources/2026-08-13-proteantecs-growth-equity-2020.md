# proteanTecs Closes $45M Growth Equity Round

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/pressroom/proteantecs-closes-45m-growth-equity-round-led-by-koch-disruptive-technologies-kdt
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: 2020-08-27

## Source excerpt

Funding to Fuel Global Expansion and Meet Growing Customer Demand

HAIFA, Israel, Aug. 27, 2020 - proteanTecs, a leading developer of Deep Data solutions for electronics' health & performance monitoring, announced today the closing of its growth equity financing round of $45M. The round was led by Koch Disruptive Technologies (KDT) and joined by Valor Equity Partners and Atreides Management, as well as existing investors. The investment will be used to accelerate proteanTecs' market penetration and scale business operations to meet growing demands.

proteanTecs introduces visibility from within. The company enables digital systems to report on their own health and performance, throughout their entire lifecycle.
