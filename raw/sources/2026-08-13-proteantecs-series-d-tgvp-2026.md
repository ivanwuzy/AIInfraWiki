# proteanTecs Receives Strategic Investment from TOPPAN Group Venture Arm TGVP

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/pressroom/proteantecs-receives-strategic-investment-from-toppan-group-venture-arm-tgvp
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: 2026-03-04

proteanTecs announced a strategic investment from TGVP, the U.S.-based corporate venture capital arm of TOPPAN. The company said the investment brought total funding to over $250 million. It described continued global growth and customer engagement across AI, cloud infrastructure, automotive, communications and consumer electronics. TOPPAN and proteanTecs were discussing future solutions for TOPPAN semiconductor customers; the amount and transaction terms were not disclosed.
