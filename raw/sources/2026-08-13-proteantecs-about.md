# proteanTecs About

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/about
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: Current company page; founding year 2017

Shai Cohen is Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of proteanTecs. Before proteanTecs, in 1999 Shai co-founded Mellanox Technologies. He held leadership roles including Chief Operations Officer from 2011. Mellanox was acquired by NVIDIA in 2019 for $7B.

Evelyn Landman is Co-Founder and Chief Technology Officer. Prior to co-founding proteanTecs in 2017, Evelyn co-founded Mellanox Technologies and served as Vice President of Backend and Product Engineering.

Roni Ashuri is Co-Founder and Chief Operations Officer. Prior to founding proteanTecs in 2017, Roni co-founded and served as Senior Vice President of Engineering at Mellanox, leading front-end chip development, silicon debug and software development for InfiniBand and Ethernet switches and endpoints.

The page describes proteanTecs as providing deep data health and performance management from silicon to system to field deployment.
