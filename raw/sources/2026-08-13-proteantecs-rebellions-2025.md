# Rebellions Advances Peta-Scale Inference with proteanTecs

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/pressroom/rebellions-advances-peta-scale-inference-with-proteantecs-deep-data-monitoring
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: 2025-10-27

proteanTecs announced that Rebellions adopted its embedded lifecycle monitoring analytics for purpose-built AI accelerators designed for inference at scale. The announcement said Rebellions gained real-time chip telemetry insights into system performance and health by embedding proteanTecs monitoring IP and using its ML-driven analytics. Rebellions CTO Jinwook Oh said visibility covered product ramp, volume production and datacenter deployment.
