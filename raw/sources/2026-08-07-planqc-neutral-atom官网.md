## Technology: Building scalable quantum computers – with nature’s best qubits.

Quantum computing demands more than big promises – it needs a scalable technology. At planqc, we build digital quantum computers based on neutral atoms, the best qubits available. Our technology is rooted in decades of cutting-edge research and designed to deliver quantum advantage to industry, science, and society.

## The planqc advantage.

### Identical qubits

Our qubits are neutral atoms that are 100% identical by nature—no fabrication defects, no variability.

### Scalable by design

Neutral atoms enable quantum systems that scale naturally from hundreds to millions of qubits—without architectural bottlenecks.

### Flexible connectivity

Atoms can be arranged in large 1D, 2D, or 3D arrays and individually manipulated using quantum gas microscopes.

### Fast quantum gates

Highly excited Rydberg states allow fast and reliable two-qubit gates using laser light.

### Extreme coherence

Atomic qubits are well isolated from their environment, maintaining quantum information for several seconds.

### Room-temperature operation

While the atoms are cooled to ultracold temperatures, the entire quantum computer runs at room temperature—no cryogenics required.

### Low energy consumption

Efficient laser cooling and compact setups make operation highly energy-efficient.

### Data center ready

planqc’s quantum computers are built to integrate seamlessly into modern data centers.
