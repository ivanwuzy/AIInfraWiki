## Selected original passages

The RISC-V open-standard instruction set architecture (ISA) defines the fundamental guidelines for designing and implementing RISC-V processors.

The RISC-V ISA specifications, extensions, and supporting documents are collaboratively developed, ratified, and maintained by contributing members of RISC-V International.

The specification library includes Ratified ISA Specifications, Ratified Extensions, and the Ratified Specifications Library.

After ratification, the published standard is stable. The original specification itself cannot be modified. Any necessary or desired modifications must be addressed through a follow-on extension. Ratified extensions are never revised.
