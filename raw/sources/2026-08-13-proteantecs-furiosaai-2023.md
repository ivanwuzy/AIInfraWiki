# FuriosaAI Enhances Next-Generation AI Chips with proteanTecs

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/pressroom/furiosaai-enhances-next-generation-ai-chips-with-proteantecs-deep-data-analytics
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: 2023-05-09

proteanTecs announced that FuriosaAI selected its deep data analytics solutions to ensure performance and field reliability of next-generation inference chips targeted at hyperscale data centers. FuriosaAI CEO June Paik said the technology would accelerate product development and provide customers with system in-field monitoring in uptime-sensitive markets.
