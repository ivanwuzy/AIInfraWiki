# Aquila

256-Qubit Analog Neutral-Atom Quantum Computer

![Image 44: Dark server rack cabinet with the word 'quEra' spelled out in small white dots and a partially open door revealing cables and illuminated equipment inside.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/643b94c382e84463a9e52264/68b97ca505984b338905b49f_aquila%20hero%20visual.png)

## A flexible, high-fidelity platform delivering research value today

Aquila is a field‑programmable, analog quantum processor built on a reconfigurable 256‑qubit neutral‑atom array. Launched in November 2022 as the first publicly accessible neutral‑atom quantum computer, it puts quantum to work across science and industry while establishing a foundation for future scalable systems.
