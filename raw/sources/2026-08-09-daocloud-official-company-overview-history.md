Founded at the end of 2014, DaoCloud is an innovation leader in the cloud native field. With the competitive expertise of its proprietary intellectual property rights, DaoCloud is committed to creating an open Cloud Operating System (Cloud OS) which enables enterprises to easily carry out digital transformation. Since its establishment, DaoCloud has been striving for business improvement in the areas such as Fintech, advanced manufacturing, intelligent vehicles, retails, and City Brain. Its benchmarking customers include Bank of Communications, Shanghai Pudong Development Bank, SAIC Motor Corporation Limited, Dongfeng Motor Corporation, Haier Group, A.S. Watson Group, and McDonald’s Corporation. DaoCloud has been reputed as a quasi-unicorn enterprise in technology.

With branches and joint ventures located in Singapore, Beijing, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Wuhan, Qingdao, Nanjing and other China’s cities. DaoCloud has over 350 employees. Reputed as National-level Specialized and Innovative "Little Giant" Enterprise, Shanghai Hi-tech Enterprise, Shanghai Emerging Tech Giant, and Shanghai Innovative and Specialized Enterprise, DaoCloud has been included in the cultivation list of the sci-tech innovation board by Shanghai Government.

Development Process:

- 2014: DaoCloud founded with a valuation of ten million US dollars and supported by famous investors from Silicon Valley, began with providing professional IT services for enterprises.
- 2015: Included in the Forrester China Public Cloud Research Report; honored with maintainers of top open-source projects; established the strategic cooperation with EMC^2.
- 2016: Obtained A funding of ten million US dollars led by Fenghe Capital; released as a container platform DCE 2.0; listed in Top 100 Asia by Red Herring.
- 2017: Obtained A+ funding of 100 million yuan led by Puhua Capital, Huatai Securities, and Aeternam Stella Financial Holdings Co., Ltd.; one of the members of the CNCF; 2017 IDC Innovator.
- 2018: Obtained B funding of 100 million yuan led by Sichuan Development Holding Co., Ltd., Yuanyi Capital, and Sichuan Tianfu Bank; established the strategic cooperation on public cloud with Microsoft and Github; the first fully CNCF qualified enterprise in China; one of core exhibitors for the World Artificial Intelligence Conferenc.
- 2019: Obtained C funding of over 100 million yuan led by Hundsun Technologies Inc. and China International Capital Corporation Limited; partnered with Pudong Development Science and Technology Commonwealth Specialized and Innovative Enterprise in Shanghai.
- 2020: Obtained D funding of over 100 million yuan led by Huatai Securities; Roby, DaoCloud CEO, was honored with a title of CNCF Ambassador; Kay Yan, DaoCloud’s Chief Architect, was listed in Forbes; 2020 Shanghai Emerging Tech Giant.
- 2021: Obtained D+ funding over 100 million yuan led by Yuxin Shicheng (Xiamen) Private Fund Management Co., Ltd.; ranked among the top nine globally in all contributions to Kubernetes; the first China’s company that joined the eBPF Foundation; listed in open source vendors and the Cloud-edge Collaboration Matrix by CAICT; signed a Memorandum of Cooperation with Huawei.
- 2022: The first multi-cluster resource retrieval project, Clusterpedia, entered the CNCF Sandbox; the only Chinese enterprise: secured a Contribution Seat on the Istio Steering Committee; Merbridge, a service mesh accelerator, has been entered for Cloud-Native Landscape; included in the 2022 Edge Computing Industry Lanscape by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT).
- 2023: Cloud-native local storage project, HwameiStor, entered the CNCF Sandbox; Roby, DaoCloud CEO, has been reappointed as an Ambassador of CNCF; launched Cloud Native Talent Development Program with Sichuan Internet Academy; established Joint Innovation Laboratory for cloud native applications with Huawei; one of the Sample Vendors for Container Management Products in China recognized by Gartner.
