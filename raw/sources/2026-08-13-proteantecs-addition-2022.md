# proteanTecs Secures $45 Million Investment from Addition

> Source: https://www.proteantecs.com/pressroom/proteantecs-secures-45-million-investment-from-addition
> Collected: 2026-08-13
> Published: 2022-05-10

proteanTecs announced that it closed a $45 million financing round with Addition. The investment was described as strengthening market leadership in deep data analytics for electronics monitoring and bringing total company funding to nearly $200 million since the company was founded in 2017. The announcement said recent investors included MediaTek, Advantest and Porsche SE.
